Prioritized business recommendations

P1 · 0–60 days
Recover rep conversion

Coach Ronnie and Wade with weekly stage reviews, call calibration, and explicit win-rate targets.

P1 · Immediate
Gate deep discounts

Require approval above 10% and measure discount-to-win lift. Extra discount is not currently buying conversion.

P2 · 30–90 days
Fix large-deal execution

Add pre-bid qualification, competitive-loss reviews, and bid playbooks for Roofing and Flooring.

P2 · 30–90 days
Protect margin

Favor higher-margin Windows & Doors opportunities while reviewing low-margin Lumber economics.

P3 · Weekly
Operate from the dashboard

Assign owners and thresholds for attainment, win rate, discount, and gross margin; review exceptions weekly.
